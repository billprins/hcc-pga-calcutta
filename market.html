How to read this: each row is one golfer. Cost is the group's full auction price (same for all 3-4 members of the group). Implied value is what this golfer would individually pay out if they finished at their current individual rank — using the same 22%/15%/12%/… placement schedule that drives the leaderboard. P/L is implied value minus the group cost — so partners outside the top 13 will show large negative P/L while a single leader can carry the whole group's potential. Use these as the floor of a negotiation, not a guaranteed price.
